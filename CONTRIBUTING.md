# Contributing to Nickel Plating Handbook

Contributions that improve operating technique, troubleshooting,
diagrams, calculations, clarity, references, or process-control
practices are welcome.

## Accepted contributions

-   Corrections
-   Better troubleshooting logic
-   Equipment/setup guidance
-   Surface-preparation guidance
-   Calculation examples
-   Process-control templates
-   Nonproprietary diagrams
-   References to recognized standards and technical literature

## Not accepted

Do not submit:

-   NickelPlatingPro proprietary formulations
-   Chemical recipes intended to reproduce commercial products
-   Proprietary ingredient lists
-   Bath-manufacturing concentrations
-   Brightener or additive formulas
-   Nickel strike recipes
-   Electroless nickel recipes
-   Proprietary replenishment chemistry
-   Confidential manufacturing procedures
-   Third-party trade secrets

## Pull requests

Explain the technical reason for the change and provide a source when
the change introduces a specific operating claim. Keep advice
chemistry-neutral unless discussing a published commercial operating
specification.
