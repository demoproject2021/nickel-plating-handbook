## Key nickel plating calculations

### Current required

`Current (A) = Surface Area (ft²) × Current Density (A/ft²)`

Example: `0.50 ft² × 30 A/ft² = 15 A`

### Ampere-hours

`Ampere-hours = Current (A) × Time (hours)`

Example: `12 A × 0.5 h = 6 Ah`

## Broad operating references

| Variable | General reference |
|---|---:|
| Nickel plating temperature | Often ~120–150°F |
| Nickel plating current density | Often ~10–50 A/ft² |
| Nickel plating pH | Commonly mildly acidic; chemistry-specific |
| Plating time | Determined by thickness, current density and verified deposition rate |

These are broad industry references. **The technical data supplied with the commercial nickel plating solution always takes priority.**

## What this handbook intentionally does not publish

No NickelPlatingPro chemical recipes, proprietary ingredient lists, bath-manufacturing concentrations, brightener ingredients, brightener formulas, nickel strike recipes, electroless nickel recipes, proprietary replenishment chemistry, or manufacturing methods are disclosed.

The goal is simple: **teach customers how to get professional results from plating chemistry—not how to manufacture competing chemistry.**
