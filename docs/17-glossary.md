# 17. Glossary

**Activation** --- Surface treatment immediately before plating intended
to remove/disrupt oxide and expose active metal.

**Ampere-hour (Ah)** --- One ampere flowing for one hour; a useful
measure of bath electrical throughput.

**Anode** --- Positive electrode in the plating cell; nickel anodes
commonly replenish nickel to the process.

**Burning** --- Dark, rough, powdery, or otherwise defective deposit
often associated with excessive local current density or inadequate mass
transfer.

**Cathode** --- Negative electrode; the workpiece being plated.

**Cathode efficiency** --- Fraction of electrical current effectively
contributing to desired metal deposition.

**Current density** --- Electrical current divided by plated surface
area.

**Degreasing** --- Removal of oils and organic soils before plating.

**Deposit** --- The plated metallic coating.

**Drag-in** --- Solution carried into a tank from the previous process
stage.

**Drag-out** --- Process solution carried out of a tank on parts/racks.

**Hull cell** --- Small test cell that produces a range of current
densities across one panel.

**Nickel strike** --- Specialized thin nickel deposit used to promote
adhesion on selected difficult/passive substrates.

**Passivation** --- Formation of a relatively unreactive surface film
that can interfere with plating adhesion.

**pH** --- Measure of acidity/alkalinity used as an operating control
variable.

**Pitting** --- Small holes/depressions in the deposit often associated
with gas, contamination, particles, or wetting problems.

**Rack** --- Conductive fixture that supports parts and carries current.

**Rectifier** --- Direct-current power supply used for electroplating.

**Satin nickel** --- Nickel finish intentionally controlled for lower
gloss/soft appearance.

**Throwing power** --- Ability of a plating system to distribute deposit
into areas receiving different current densities.

**Water-break test** --- Cleanliness test in which water should form a
continuous film rather than bead or retract.
