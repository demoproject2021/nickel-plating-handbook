# 19. Suggested Diagrams for the Repository

These diagrams can be added later as SVG files under `/assets/`.

## Diagram 1 --- Nickel plating cell

Show: - Rectifier positive lead to nickel anodes - Rectifier negative
lead to workpiece - Tank - Electrolyte - Heater - Filter pickup and
return - Solution-flow arrows

## Diagram 2 --- Current distribution

Show a rectangular part facing an anode with denser current lines at
edges/corners and fewer lines in recesses.

## Diagram 3 --- Water-break test

Side-by-side: - PASS: continuous water sheet - FAIL: beading/islands

## Diagram 4 --- Rack and anode geometry

Top view showing centered work, balanced anodes, consistent spacing, and
a bad example with one anode too close.

## Diagram 5 --- Troubleshooting flowchart

Convert the text tree in `14-decision-tree.md` into a GitHub-friendly
SVG.

## Diagram 6 --- Process line

`Degrease → Clean → Rinse → Activate → Strike if required → Nickel → Rinse → Dry/inspect`

## Diagram rules

Do not include proprietary chemical compositions, product ingredient
callouts, formulation concentrations, manufacturing instructions, or
additive recipes in diagrams.
