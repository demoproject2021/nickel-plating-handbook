# 18. References and Further Reading

Use authoritative metal-finishing references, chemistry-supplier
technical data, equipment manuals, and applicable industry standards.

Recommended reference categories:

-   ASTM standards applicable to electrodeposited nickel coatings and
    thickness testing
-   ASM Handbook volumes covering surface engineering
-   Modern Electroplating and related electrochemical engineering texts
-   Metal Finishing Guidebook / finishing-industry handbooks
-   Manufacturer technical data sheets for the exact commercial nickel
    solution, strike, additives, cleaners, activators, and equipment in
    use
-   Calibrated instrument manuals for pH, temperature, thickness, and
    electrical measurement

## Source priority

For operating a commercial bath, use this order:

1.  Exact product technical data sheet
2.  Supplier technical support
3.  Applicable customer/engineering specification
4.  Recognized industry standard
5.  General handbook guidance

Generic internet recipes should never override the specification for the
chemistry actually in the tank.
